# Release v1.0.0 Stable 🚀

We are thrilled to announce the official release of **JadwalNih v1.0.0 Stable**! This release brings a complete productivity and lifestyle suite designed for Android.

## 🌟 What's New in v1.0.0
- **Unified Calendar & Monthly Grid**: View all tasks, red holidays, national observances, birthdays, and photo recaps in one central calendar tab.
- **Interactive Map & Location Pinpointing**: Pinpoint locations directly on maps and launch Google Maps seamlessly.
- **Smart Worship & Prayer Cards**: Contextual prayer cards that appear during natural prayer windows with check-in history and manual override options.
- **Birthday & Usage Stats**: Full-screen birthday celebration theme with cake, candles, balloons, confetti, and usage statistics summary.
- **Immersive Holiday Atmosphere & Motion Graphics**: Thematic wallpaper backgrounds and one-time opening motion animations for Independence Day, Eid, Imlek, Christmas, and Waisak.
- **Robust Local & Cloud Persistence**: Powered by Room Database and Jetpack DataStore with JSON backup and restore capabilities.
